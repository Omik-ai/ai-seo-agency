Yes — an agent can help a lot with backlink acquisition for iamamitkumar.dev, but it should work like a research + outreach assistant, not a spam bot. Google explicitly treats buying/selling links for ranking, excessive link exchanges, and using automated programs/services to create links as link spam. Google also recommends marking paid placements with rel="sponsored" and many community/user-generated links with rel="ugc", so the goal is earned, relevant, editorial links, not mass drops. (developers.google.com)

For your site specifically, you already have strong “linkable angles”: your build-in-public founder story, LaunchSuite.tech, VidoTask, founder notes/newsletter, sponsor page, and the “Build + GTM” offer. Those are much more useful than generic “please link to my portfolio” outreach because they give publishers a real reason to mention you. (iamamitkumar.dev)

The big mistake to avoid: don’t choose targets mainly by DR. Choose by:

Topical relevance to AI / indie hacking / SaaS / product / growth
Editorial control — can a real editor or community member choose to feature you?
Audience fit — does the link send the kind of people who would read, share, buy, or collaborate?
Link type — editorial mention > profile link > comment/forum drop.
Google does use links as a signal for discovery/relevance, but it also emphasizes helpful, people-first content, so relevance beats random high-DR placements. (developers.google.com)

Can an agent do this well?
Yes — very well, if you scope it correctly.

Good tasks for an agent
prospecting domains and authors
scraping contact pages / submission guidelines
classifying targets by relevance
drafting personalized outreach
turning one post into multiple pitch angles
finding unlinked mentions
tracking outreach status
suggesting internal links and anchor opportunities
Bad tasks for an agent
auto-posting on forums/comments at scale
buying links
reciprocal-link spam
fake personas / fake testimonials
spinning guest posts and mass-submitting them
submitting to irrelevant directories just because DR is high
That last category is exactly where you can drift into link spam territory. Google explicitly warns against automated link creation and manipulative link schemes. (developers.google.com)
