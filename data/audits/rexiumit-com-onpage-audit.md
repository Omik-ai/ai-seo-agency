## Critical Issues (P0)

1. **Missing Meta Descriptions** — All pages lack meta descriptions
2. **No Schema Markup** — Zero structured data across entire site
3. **No FAQ Sections** — Missing FAQ schema opportunity on all service pages
4. **No E-E-A-T Signals** — No author bios, team credentials, client testimonials on content pages
5. **Title Tag Over-Optimization** — Multiple titles exceed 60 characters, brand repetition

---

## High Priority (P1)

1. **Heading Spacing Issues** — Multiple H1/H2 have concatenated words (e.g., "AI-First DevelopmentThat")
2. **Missing Internal Links** — Limited contextual internal linking between pages
3. **Blog Not Functional** — Shows "Loading..." indicating potential SSR issue
4. **No Image Alt Text** — Logo and feature images need proper alt attributes
5. **Missing FAQ Schema on Pricing** — High-value SERP real estate opportunity

---

## Medium Priority (P2)

1. **Inconsistent Title Tag Format** — Mix of with/without brand suffix
2. **Missing OG Tags** — No Open Graph metadata for social sharing
3. **No Canonical URLs** — Need verification for duplicate content
4. **Limited LSI Keywords** — Content could incorporate more semantic variations
5. **No Sitemap** — XML sitemap not verified

---

## Quick Wins

1. **Add Meta Descriptions** — Write compelling 150-160 char descriptions with CTAs
2. **Fix Title Tags** — Shorten to 50-60 chars, remove brand duplication
3. **Add FAQ Schema** — Create FAQ sections on Services, Pricing, Contact pages
4. **Add Organization Schema** — Homepage needs Organization + LocalBusiness
5. **Add ContactPage Schema** — Contact page needs contactPoint markup
6. **Add Breadcrumb Schema** — All main navigation pages
7. **Fix H1 Spacing** — Add proper spaces in heading text
8. **Add E-E-A-T Elements** — Team page with bios, client testimonials

---

## Recommendations
