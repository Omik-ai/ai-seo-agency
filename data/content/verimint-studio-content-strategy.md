## E-E-A-T Signal Strategy

### Experience
- Include real project examples in case studies
- Show before/after content transformations
- Share specific client results and metrics
- Include author bylines with real experience credentials

### Expertise
- Author bios with academic credentials and industry experience
- Cite academic sources and research in content
- Include expert quotes and interviews
- Demonstrate deep knowledge of academic standards (APA, MLA, Chicago)

### Authoritativeness
- Publish data-driven reports and industry studies
- Get quoted in academic and marketing publications
- Build backlinks from educational institutions
- Create guest posts for authoritative publications

### Trust
- Transparent pricing and process
- Client testimonials with verifiable results
- Clear contact information and company details
- Privacy policy, terms of service, cookie policy
- SSL certificate and security badges
- Real team photos and bios

### Internal Link Rules
1. Every blog post links back to its pillar page (services)
2. Every blog post links to 2-3 related blog posts
3. Every blog post includes a CTA linking to /services or /pricing
4. Service pages link to relevant blog content
5. Homepage links to top 3 blog posts
6. Maximum 3 clicks from any page to any other page
