## Template Guidelines

### Do:
- Personalize every email — mention specific details about their site
- Keep it concise — 3-5 short paragraphs max
- Provide clear value — explain why your resource helps their audience
- Include a single, clear call-to-action
- Follow up once after 7 days if no response
- Use a professional email signature

### Don't:
- Send bulk, generic emails
- Ask for a link directly — ask to be considered as a resource
- Follow up more than twice
- Use aggressive or pushy language
- Send to irrelevant sites
- Include more than 2-3 links in one email
