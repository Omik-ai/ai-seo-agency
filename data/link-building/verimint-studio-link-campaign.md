## Campaign Tactics

### Tactic 1: Resource Page Link Building
- **Target**: 50 university writing center resource pages, library guides, academic support pages
- **Approach**: Email webmasters/librarians suggesting Verimint Studio as a premium academic content resource
- **Expected Success Rate**: 10-15%
- **Timeline**: 4-8 weeks
- **Outreach Template**: See outreach-templates/resource-page-outreach.md

### Tactic 2: Guest Posting
- **Target**: 20 marketing blogs, academic writing blogs, education technology sites
- **Approach**: Pitch expert articles on academic writing, content marketing, and the intersection of both
- **Expected Success Rate**: 20-30%
- **Timeline**: 8-12 weeks
- **Outreach Template**: See outreach-templates/guest-post-pitch.md

### Tactic 3: HARO/Connectively Responses
- **Target**: 3-5 journalist queries per week
- **Approach**: Respond to queries about content marketing, academic writing, education, and business communication
- **Expected Success Rate**: 5-10%
- **Timeline**: Ongoing
- **Outreach Template**: See outreach-templates/haro-response.md

### Tactic 4: Broken Link Building
- **Target**: 20 broken links on academic and marketing resource pages
- **Approach**: Find broken links, notify site owner, suggest Verimint content as replacement
- **Expected Success Rate**: 15-20%
- **Timeline**: 4-6 weeks
- **Outreach Template**: See outreach-templates/broken-link.md

### Tactic 5: Directory Submissions
- **Target**: 40+ relevant directories (academic, marketing, business)
- **Approach**: Submit to high-quality, relevant directories
- **Expected Success Rate**: 70-80%
- **Timeline**: 2-3 weeks
- **Outreach Template**: Not needed — form submissions

### Tactic 6: Unlinked Brand Mention Reclamation
- **Target**: Ongoing monitoring
- **Approach**: Set up Google Alerts for "Verimint Studio" and "Verimint", request links for unlinked mentions
- **Expected Success Rate**: 50-70%
- **Timeline**: Ongoing
