## Executive Summary

Verimint Studio is a premium academic content and content marketing agency positioned to serve clients across three major English-speaking markets. The website (verimint.studio) has completed UI/UX development (Phases 1-10) with 6 core pages and a professional design system. **Zero SEO infrastructure exists** — this represents both a gap and an opportunity.

The SEO strategy focuses on three pillars:
1. **Technical Foundation** — Build search-engine-ready infrastructure (sitemap, schema, metadata, performance)
2. **Topical Authority** — Dominate academic content and content marketing niches through strategic content clusters
3. **Authority Building** — Earn high-quality backlinks from academic, marketing, and business publications

**Expected timeline to first measurable results**: 60-90 days
**Expected timeline to significant organic traffic**: 6-9 months
**Target**: 10,000+ monthly organic sessions within 12 months
