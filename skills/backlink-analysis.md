### 6. Toxic Link Assessment
Criteria for toxic classification:
- From known spam domains
- From PBN patterns (same IP, similar content, interlinking)
- Sitewide footer/sidebar links
- From irrelevant foreign language sites
- Unnatural anchor text patterns
- From penalized domains
